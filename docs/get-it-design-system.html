Effort / Restore switch

The whole app has two moods. Ember for training, Fern for mobility, breathwork, walks and sleep. The thumb springs across so the mode change feels physical.

Primary button

Pill, Ember, full width, always in thumb reach. Pressing scales it to 96% and fires an amber spark from the touch point. A short haptic tick on phones.

Hold-to-log ring

A tap is too easy to hit by accident mid-set. Holding for 0.7 s fills the ring; letting go early cancels. Logging turns the row Fern. A record turns the ring Amber and says so.

Swipe-to-complete rows

Drag a set row to the right past 40% and it locks in. Under it is Moss, so completing a set reveals the recovery colour. Drag back to undo.

Rest timer that breathes

The number swells and settles on a four-second cycle, a cue to breathe. Buttons shift by 15 s. It runs in the background and vibrates at zero.

Muscle chips

Selected chips glow Amber, the same light as the Coach figure's working muscles, so the chip and the video speak the same language.

Tab bar

Five tabs. The Ember indicator overshoots and settles like a cable on a pulley. Restore has its own tab: the holistic side is not buried in settings.

Motion and material rules

Hand these to Base44 with the palette. They are what keep the app from feeling like a template.

Timing200 to 350 ms, ease-out. Springs only on the segmented thumb, the tab indicator, and the match of a record badge.
PressEvery button scales to 96% while pressed and returns in 160 ms. Primary buttons also fire the amber spark.
Colour by moodEmber means effort and action. Fern and Moss mean recovery and completion. Amber means light: muscle glow, records, selection. Signal is reserved for over-target and missed days.
CornersPills for actions, 22 px for cards, 14 px for rows. Never one radius everywhere.
TextureCards carry a 3% grain overlay in dark mode so surfaces feel like stone, not plastic. No gradients except the amber glow.
TypeBricolage Grotesque 800 for headlines and big numbers, tabular figures. Manrope for everything else. Uppercase labels at 11 px with 0.12 em tracking.
Light modeSand ground with Graphite text: the outdoor version. Same accents, same rules.
NeverConfetti, cartoon badges, red-green traffic lights, full-screen paywall on open, default component styling, a spinner longer than 400 ms.
