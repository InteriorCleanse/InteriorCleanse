# Clipping campaign: paying creators per 1,000 views

The distribution half of `docs/MARKETING_TEAM.md`, taken from the clipping
model in the Jack Neel reel the owner chose (2026-10-16). Brands post a
campaign on **Whop Content Rewards**. Independent clippers cut short videos
from the brand's footage, post them on their own accounts, and are paid a set
rate for every 1,000 views on clips the brand approves. It works like ad
spend, except the money goes to creators and is only paid for views that
happened.

The form fields below are the ones Whop's own documentation lists. Whop's fee
is not stated in its docs, and third-party reports disagree, so check the
figure on the funding screen before depositing.

## Before launching: two gates

1. **The shop can take money.** Stripe is live and a real test purchase has
   been refunded (`docs/FINISH_PLAN.md`). Paying for views that land on a shop
   that can't sell is wasted money.
2. **There is enough footage.** Clippers need source material. Today there are
   five 8-second room films and the 3D product viewers, which is thin. Before
   launch, add at least three longer founder videos (5–15 minutes each, phone
   is fine): an evening reset in a real room, setting a table with the mug and
   candle, and reading from one of the books. Real footage clips better than
   generated footage, and the clippers' best material comes from people
   talking.

## The form, ready to paste

Whop → **Add app** → **Content Rewards** → **Add**, then fill in:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign name | InteriorCleanse — calm-home clips, $1 per 1K views |
| Campaign type | **Clipping** (the footage is ours). Switch to **UGC** later, when creators can have the product in hand |
| Brand category | Home / lifestyle (pick the closest Whop offers) |
| Campaign budget | **Set by the owner.** A first test is commonly a few hundred dollars. At $1 per 1,000 views, $100 buys up to 100,000 approved views |
| Reward rate | $1.00 per 1,000 views. Live campaigns mostly pay $0.50–$1.50 for clipping |
| Minimum payout | $1 (a clip must reach 1,000 views before it is reviewed, which saves review time) |
| Maximum payout | $50 per clip, so one viral clip cannot spend the whole budget |
| Flat fee bonus | None for the first campaign |
| Platforms | TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| Available content | Link to a Google Doc made from the clipper kit below |
| Requirements | Paste the list below |

Then **Add budget**, choose a payment method, enter the amount, and **Send**.
The campaign moves from *Pending budget* to *Active* in about a minute. Every
submission still needs the owner's approval before Whop pays it.

## Requirements (paste into the form)

- Use only footage and images from the InteriorCleanse kit. No other brand's
  footage, no people who did not agree to appear.
- Disclose the payment on every clip: the platform's paid-partnership label, or
  #ad in the first line of the caption. Clips without it are rejected (this is
  an FTC requirement for paid endorsements, not a style choice).
- Show or say interiorcleanse.com.
- Vertical 9:16, 10 to 60 seconds, captions burned in.
- Calm, warm and honest in tone. No shouting hooks, no fake urgency, no
  "limited stock", no invented prices, discounts or reviews.
- No health, cleaning-efficacy or medical claims about any product.
- Respectful handling of the faith books; no mockery.
- Music only from the platform's own licensed library.
- No bought views, bots or reposting another clipper's edit. Accounts that do
  this are removed.

**Hooks that fit the brand.** These are angles, not scripts:
- "The five-minute evening reset that changed how my mornings feel."
- "Three things on my nightstand, and why there are only three."
- "What a calm room actually needs (it's less than you think)."
- "Setting the table like it matters, on a Tuesday."

**Never say:** that a product cures, cleans, detoxes or heals anything; that
stock is running out; any price other than the one on the site that day.

## Running it

| When | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| Daily, first week | owner | Approve or reject submissions (10 minutes) |
| Friday | `/marketing-team scorecard` | Views paid for, spend, cost per 1,000 views, orders from clip traffic |
| After the first budget | owner | Keep, raise the rate for better clippers, or stop |

Judge it on orders, not views. Clip traffic is cold, so the guest book and the
free Room Reset Checklist matter more than the product pages.
