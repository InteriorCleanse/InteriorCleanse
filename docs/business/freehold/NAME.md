# The name

Chosen 2026-09-27 for the theme the owner set: an AI app builder that is
better than Base44 because the customer owns everything it makes, sold with
discretion to people who buy on trust, and a private-software practice for
family offices and the professionals who serve them. Domain availability was
checked live through Vercel's registrar on 2026-09-27; "taken" means the
registrar reported it unavailable, which includes premium listings.

## Recommended: Freehold

**Freehold** is the property-law word for owning something outright, with no
landlord and no lease that can end. It is the exact opposite of a hosted
builder, where the app lives on someone else's land. It is one word, English,
two syllables, easy to say on a call, and it reads as old money rather than
startup. A family office chief of staff understands it instantly.

- Tagline: *Software you own outright.*
- Builder product: **Freehold Build** (the app builder; your repo, your
  database, your key).
- Private practice: **Freehold Private** (bespoke software for family
  offices and the people who run them).
- Wordmark: FREEHOLD in a wide, quiet serif; the mark is a single unbroken
  square outline, the boundary line of a plot you own.

Domains available at the time of checking:

| Domain | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `freeholdprivate.com` | The private practice, and the company site if one name must carry both |
| `freehold.build` | The app builder |
| `freeholdandco.com` | Alternative company domain if the owner prefers a .com for everything |
| `freeholdatelier.com` | Alternative for the private practice |
| `freehold.partners`, `freeholdprivate.co`, `freeholdprivate.dev` | Defensive registrations |

Taken: `freehold.dev`, `freehold.co`, `freehold.com` (not checked, assumed),
`freeholdsoftware.com`, `freehold.studio`, `freehold.systems`, and most
plain compounds. The single-word .com is not available to a new company, so
the brand should be spoken as "Freehold" and typed as `freeholdprivate.com`
or `freehold.build` from the first day, the way many firms carry a suffix.

The word is common in UK and Commonwealth property, so a trademark search in
the software class (Nice class 42) is the owner's next step before printing
anything. It is not a conflict check this document can perform.

## Runners-up

| Name | Why it works | Why it lost | Available |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Keyholder** | The person who holds the keys to a house; the customer holds the API key and the repo | Reads as security or locksmith at first glance; the .com is taken | `keyholder.studio`, `keyholder.build`, `keyholderworks.com`, `keyholderstudio.com` |
| **Deed** | The document that proves ownership; short and sharp | Too short to search for; only the .build TLD is free | `deed.build` |
| **Clearhold** | Coined; "clear title" plus "hold"; sounds like a firm | Means nothing until explained | `clearhold.dev` |
| **Ownside** | "On your side" plus "own"; friendly | Too clever, and .com taken | `ownside.dev` |

## What the name unlocks

Once the owner confirms Freehold (or picks a runner-up), Phase 1 of
`docs/BUSINESS_BUILD_PLAN.md` starts: the brand voice, three logo
directions generated for the owner to choose from, the SVG mark, tokens, and
then the website on its own repository and Vercel project. Nothing is
purchased by an agent; the domains above are the owner's to buy.
