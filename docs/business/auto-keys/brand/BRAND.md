# KeyRaptor — brand

A separate project from InteriorCleanse. This folder stages the brand until
the business gets its own repository and Vercel project, as the build plan
requires.

## The name

**KeyRaptor.** A raptor is fast and apex. That is the promise: a key cut,
coded, and in your hand faster than anyone, wherever you are. It is the one
name that stands clear of the field, which is a wall of near-identical
descriptive marks (KeyMe, Tom's Key, InstaCarKey, Keyless Entry Remote,
Pop-A-Lock). It is a coined word, so it is ownable and defensible, and it
renders into a sharp mark that suits the cyber interface.

Tagline: **Cut · Coded · Anywhere.**

The mark is a key — bow, eye, shaft, teeth, pointed tip — whose head carries
a raptor's eye and beak. It is drawn with clipped corners to echo the login
card. Before launch, export the icon to PNG at 512, 1024, and 3000, a 32px
and 180px favicon, and a 1200×630 social image. Keep clear space around the
lockup equal to the height of the icon badge.

## Type

- **Display and wordmark:** a heavy geometric sans, tight tracking, uppercase
  (the lockup uses a system heavy sans as a stand-in; license a real face
  such as a grotesk or a technical sans before launch).
- **Interface and body:** a monospace for labels, codes, prices, and status
  readouts, which carries the terminal feel. A clean sans for long copy.

## Voice

Confident, terse, technical, never sketchy. The industry's reputation is
bait-and-switch; the brand's entire tone is the opposite of that.

- Say: flat price, verified, on the way, no surprises, we come to you.
- Never say: a price we will not honour, "starting at", or any claim to be a
  dealer or the manufacturer.
- Three words: **fast, honest, precise.**

## Why this beats the category on looks alone

Every competitor's site looks like a 2012 service directory. A dark,
motion-rich, neon interface signals a technology company, and trust in this
trade is won or lost on first impression. The design is the moat's first wall.
