# What Picked takes from Bloom Nutrition

Bloom is the closest model for what Picked wants to become: a flavor-led
supplement brand built on short video and a real founder, that went from
online-only to Target, Walmart, Costco, and Amazon. It also shows what to
avoid, because most of its public trouble came from claims and labels.

**How this was researched:** web search only, October 2026. The research
environment blocked YouTube and most article pages, so no video transcript
was read. Every fact below comes from the cited page's search excerpt. Items
marked *unverified* could not be confirmed. Watch the founders' own
interviews yourself (search "Mari Llewellyn Greg LaVecchia interview") before
quoting them.

## The story in eight lines

- **2019:** Mari Llewellyn and Greg LaVecchia start Bloom in Venice,
  California, self-funded. Before that, Llewellyn sold workout guides and
  resistance bands. First product: a pre-workout
  ([Kara Goldin Show](https://karagoldin.com/podcasts/mari-llewellyn/),
  [BiggerPockets](https://www.biggerpockets.com/blog/money-482)).
- **About 2021:** Greens & Superfoods takes off on TikTok, driven by
  creators ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/bloom-nutrition-mari-llewellyn-12-billion-views-on-tiktok/)).
- **October 2022:** first retail, 600+ Target stores. Online-only until then
  ([Taste Radio](https://www.tasteradio.com/episodes/why-bloom-is-booming-on-tiktok-and-winning-at-target-walmart)).
- **February 2023:** about 1,800 Walmart stores (same source).
- **January 2024:** Nutrabolt (maker of C4) takes about 20% in a $90M round;
  founders keep control ([Nutrition Insight](https://www.nutritioninsight.com/news/nutrabolt-purchases-20-of-bloom-nutrition-to-propel-growth-and-innovation-in-wellness-supplements.html)).
- **July 2024:** Sparkling Energy launches as a Target exclusive and passes
  $100M in its first year ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/his-new-product-sold-more-than-100-million-in-one-year-heres-his-strategy)).
- **2025:** prebiotic soda, creatine, Ulta. Nutrabolt takes a majority stake
  for about $210M in total ([BevNET](https://www.bevnet.com/news/2025/nutrabolt-takes-majority-stake-in-bloom-nutrition/)).
- **Revenue:** reported between about $170M and over $300M depending on the
  source and year ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/meganbruneau/2026/02/08/the-unsexy-habit-that-turned-a-college-dropout-into-a-300-million-founder/)).

## Where Bloom got hurt, and Picked's rule

| Bloom's problem | Source | Picked's rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Symptom claims.** Founder content about "bloat" and hangovers was flagged as unapproved drug claims | [TINA.org](https://truthinadvertising.org/articles/bloom-nutrition-greens-superfoods-powder/) | No body, symptom, or health claims at all. Taste, fruit, protein grams, testing |
| **Proprietary blends.** 38 ingredients in blends; critics said doses were too small to matter | [Wellness Pulse](https://wellnesspulse.com/nutrition/bloom-greens-superfoods-controversy/) | No blends. Every ingredient disclosed, fruit in grams on the front |
| **No published test results**, so reviewers questioned quality | [Wellness Pulse](https://wellnesspulse.com/nutrition/bloom-greens-superfoods-controversy/) | The Lot Book: every lot's results, public |
| **Thin creator disclosure** | [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/news/bloom-greens-advertisements/) | #ad or "gifted" on every creator post, checked by `brand-guardian` |
| **Label lawsuits:** a colostrum potency class action, and a "no artificial flavors" suit over citric acid (outcomes *unverified*) | [Juris Law Group](https://jurislawgroup.com/class-actions-lawsuits-newsletter-september-2025/), [Top Class Actions](https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/class-action-claims-bloom-sparkling-energy-drinks-contain-artificial-flavor/) | Every label word checked against the actual formula by a label reviewer. Say "no sucralose", not "nothing artificial" |
| **High-caffeine cans popular with teens** | [Hillsborough Voice](https://hillsborough-voice.com/15195/showcase/con-bloom-should-not-be-sold-in-school/) | No stimulant products. No marketing to under-18s |

## The ten things to do now

1. **Film yourself every weekday.** The founder on camera is the cheapest,
   most copied, least copyable part of Bloom's engine.
2. **Write your real story** using the prompts in `BRAND_PLATFORM.md`. Nobody
   else writes it.
3. **Pick one series and repeat it.** "The Real Fruit Test" label reads are
   Picked's version of a recurring hook.
4. **Build the creator list now,** product later. 100 micro-creators in the
   tracker before the first pouch exists.
5. **Seed widely, brief tightly.** Free product with no obligation, the
   approved claims list, disclosure always.
6. **Let people vote.** Every flavor after Strawberry is chosen by First Picks.
7. **Make the pack the ad.** The fruit number must be readable in a phone
   video from arm's length.
8. **Measure reorders, not views.** The 45-day repeat rate is the number that
   decides whether to scale.
9. **Sell online first, then one store at a time.** Retail only with proof
   you can move product.
10. **Publish what Bloom didn't.** Grams, ingredients, and lot results. That's
    the gap Picked is built to fill.
