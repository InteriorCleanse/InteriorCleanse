# Picked strategy

## The sentence

Picked is a whey protein drink that tastes like the fruit on the label,
because the fruit on the label is in it, and the pack says how many grams.

## Who buys it first

People who already spend $40 to $60 a month on protein, drink it with water
after training or as a mid-afternoon snack, and are bored of chocolate and
vanilla or put off by candy-tasting "fruit" proteins. Runners, lifters,
pilates and spin regulars, and people raising their protein intake for any
reason. Marketing can reach people on GLP-1 medications who want light,
non-milky protein, but Picked makes no medical claim of any kind.

## Why we can win

| What buyers dislike now | What Picked does |
| --- | --- |
| "Tastes like an orange-flavored antibiotic" | Real fruit is the main flavor ingredient |
| Sucralose and Ace-K aftertaste | Monk fruit and/or stevia only |
| "Natural flavors" hide what is in it | Grams of fruit per scoop on the front |
| Heavy-metal worries in protein powder | Every lot's lab results behind a QR code |

The closest competitor is Just Ingredients Clear at $59.99 for 20 servings. It
is a premium wellness brand with a small range. Picked is brighter, cheaper
per serving, and puts the fruit number on the front. Sources in `RESEARCH.md`.

**What this means**

1. Sell on your own website first. It is the only channel with a healthy margin today.
2. In stores, start with stick packs and a few direct accounts. Keep
   distributors for later.
3. Go to distributors only once the cost of goods is $16 or less. That comes
   from larger runs, from whey prices falling, or from both.
4. A customer is worth what they leave over their whole time with you.
   Ad spend per first order must stay under about $22 until subscriptions prove out.

## First channel

**Your own Shopify store, fed by a waitlist and by in-person sampling.** A
cup of Strawberry Picked handed out at a run club finish line is the cheapest
ad this product will ever have. Then Faire for local stores, then Amazon once
the trademark is filed and the manufacturer passes Amazon's cGMP check.

## Offer and lead magnet

- **Offer:** founding customers get 20% off their first pouch and a free
  shaker for joining the waitlist.
- **Lead magnet:** *The Real Fruit Test.* A one-page guide to reading a
  protein label: what "natural flavors", "WONF", and "made with real fruit"
  actually mean. It teaches the buyer to see why Picked is different.
