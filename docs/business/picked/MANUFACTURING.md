## A risk to settle on the first call

**Pure Private Label's protein capability is not confirmed.** Its published
lines are electrolytes, creatine, greens, and collagen. The lean launch in
`TIMELINE.md` (144 pouches for about $5k to $8k) depends on it making a whey
drink mix. If it can't:

- Use the $695 program to develop the **hydration sticks** (Picked's second
  product), and
- Make the protein at NutraSeller. Its own guidance is $10 to $20 a unit for
  custom work at small scale, so 2,500 pouches is roughly **$25k to $50k**
  (an estimate; the quote replaces it). That moves the first run onto the
  standard path's budget.

**Protein in a stick pack may not fit.** A 20 g protein serving of clear whey
with fruit is about 25 to 30 g of powder. Most stick lines top out around
25 g. Ask each manufacturer for its maximum stick fill. If it can't fit, the
single-serve format becomes a wider sachet, or the sample stays a hydration
stick.

run. Hydration sticks come second, at StickPax (7,500) or Pure Private Label
(144), until volume justifies moving them under the main partner.

## Ten questions for every discovery call

1. Have you made an acidified clear whey isolate commercially? What pH,
   clarity, and foam control do you use? Will freeze-dried fruit cloud it?
2. What is your maximum stick fill weight, and what stick width would a
   25 to 30 g serving need?
3. Is the minimum per flavor, per formula, or per SKU? Can one run split
   across flavors?
4. Are blending, pouch filling, and stick filling all in your own facility,
   or is any of it subcontracted?
5. What does flavor development cost, how many rounds are included, and is
   the fee credited against the first order?
6. Do you hold NSF/ANSI 455-2? Can you run specific batches under NSF
   Certified for Sport or Informed Sport?
7. Do you source freeze-dried fruit, or do we supply it? How do you control
   water activity and caking with fruit powder?
8. What is the all-in cost per unit at your minimum: packaging, labels,
   testing, COA, and freight? What deposit and payment terms?
9. What stability and shelf-life testing do you run, and how long do you
   keep retained samples?
10. Can we speak to two brands that launched a clear whey or real-fruit
    hydration product with you?

**Packaging tip:** whey and fruit powder both pull in moisture. Ask the
printer for the film's moisture and oxygen barrier figures, and fill a test
pouch with the real powder before ordering at scale.

**Fruit tip:** if you supply the fruit, ask for its COA, micro spec, and
allergen statement first. The manufacturer will need them to accept it.
