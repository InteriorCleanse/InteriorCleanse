# Picked product copy

**Status:** pre-launch draft. Nothing here is final until the formula, the
Supplement Facts, and the label review are done.

## Label consultant: review before print

1. **Category line "REAL FRUIT PROTEIN DRINK MIX".** "Drink" can push a product
   toward food (FDA 2014 guidance). Confirm it works with a dietary supplement
   statement of identity, or give us replacement wording. If it changes, update
   every place it appears in this file.
2. **Statement of identity.** "Dietary Supplement" is a placeholder until the
   regulatory route is fixed.
3. **Flavor names under 21 CFR 101.22(i).** We are aiming for plain
   "Strawberry", "Mango", and "Raspberry Lemon". Confirm once the fruit load and
   any added flavor are known.
4. **"no fake fruit" and "No fake fruit flavor."** True only if the final
   formula has no artificial flavor and any natural flavor comes from the named
   fruit. Confirm or reword.
5. **Stick packs.** Confirm which label elements fit on the stick and which can
   move to the box.
6. **Prop 65.** Decide with the first lot results in hand.
