# Email to a trademark attorney

## Finding one

- **Your state bar's lawyer referral service.** Ask for a trademark attorney
  who works on flat fees for consumer products.
- **Flat-fee trademark firms.** Many quote one price for a clearance search
  plus filing. Ask who does the search (an attorney, or a database printout
  with no opinion).
- **The USPTO's law school clinic program.** Free help from supervised law
  students for eligible applicants, but slower. Search "USPTO Law School
  Clinic Certification Program".
- **Avoid** anyone who files without a clearance search, or companies that
  only fill in forms and give no legal opinion.

Ask two or three attorneys and compare the fixed fees. USPTO filing fees are
separate: $350 per class.

- `brand/png/picked-wordmark-1024.png` (the logo)

**Subject:** Trademark clearance and filing for PICKED (protein and drink mixes)

Hi [attorney name],

I'm launching a consumer brand called **PICKED** and would like a clearance
opinion and, if the name is clear, help filing. I'd like to get a quote for
the work below.

**The brand:** a whey protein drink mix flavored with real fruit, sold online
and in stores in the US. Launch is planned for early 2027. The logo is the
lowercase word "picked" with a leaf over the i (attached, with a packaging
concept).

**Goods, now and planned:**

- Protein powder and protein drink mixes (sold as dietary supplements)
- Electrolyte and hydration drink mixes
- Creatine, collagen, and greens powders
- Later, a ready-to-drink protein beverage

**What I'm asking for:**

1. A clearance search and written opinion on PICKED for these goods. I think
   the relevant classes are 5 (dietary supplements), 32 (powders for making
   beverages, and beverages), and possibly 29 and 30. Please tell me which
   classes you'd recommend.
2. If PICKED looks risky, a quick check of my backup name, **BUSHEL**, for
   the same goods.
3. If it's clear, an intent-to-use application for the word mark in the
   classes you recommend. Should the logo be filed separately, now or later?

**Questions:**

1. My company is being formed now. Should I file in the company's name once
   it exists, or file now in my own name and assign it later?
2. Is a single common word like "picked" strong enough to register for these
   goods, or would you expect a descriptiveness or crowded-field problem?
3. I plan to sell on Amazon, which needs a pending application for Brand
   Registry. How soon after filing would I have a serial number?
4. What is your flat fee for the search and opinion, and for each filing? And
   roughly how long until I have your opinion? I want to approve packaging in
   early December, so I'd ideally have an answer before then.

Thanks very much. Happy to talk by phone if that's quicker.
