What we need in the quote

  1. Unit price at each quantity, with protein, fruit, packaging, tolling, and freight shown as separate lines.
  2. Flavor development fee, number of bench rounds included, and sample timing.
  3. Minimum order per flavor and whether flavors can share one protein lot.
  4. Lead time from approved samples to finished goods.
  5. Certifications held: cGMP (21 CFR 111), NSF or equivalent GMP audit, organic, kosher, halal.
  6. Testing included per lot: identity, protein content, microbiology, and heavy metals (lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury). Is a certificate of analysis supplied?
  7. Shelf-life or stability testing offered, and its price.
  8. Whether you can produce batches eligible for NSF Certified for Sport or Informed Sport.
  9. Whether you help with the Supplement Facts panel and label review.
  10. Payment terms.

What we will not accept

  • A stock "fruit punch" or "berry blast" base with a fruit name swapped in.
  • Any claim of fruit content we cannot back with a batch record.
  • Sucralose or acesulfame K, even "just a little".
