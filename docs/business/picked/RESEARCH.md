## Rules that shape the label

- Supplement vs food follows intended use ([foodlabelmaker](https://help.foodlabelmaker.com/en/articles/15261813-when-should-you-use-a-nutrition-facts-label-versus-a-supplement-facts-label)). Supplement route: 21 CFR 111 cGMP, which the brand owner cannot contract away ([FDA guide](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/small-entity-compliance-guide-current-good-manufacturing-practice-manufacturing-packaging-labeling)); structure/function claims need the FDA disclaimer and a 30-day notice ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/information-industry-dietary-supplements/notifications-structurefunction-and-related-claims-dietary-supplement-labeling)); a US address or phone for adverse events ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/information-consumers-using-dietary-supplements/questions-and-answers-dietary-supplements)).
- "Contains: Milk" under FALCPA ([NALC](https://nationalaglawcenter.org/food-labeling-the-regulation-of-food-allergen-labels/)).
- Flavour naming, 21 CFR 101.22(i): enough real fruit to carry the taste allows plain "Strawberry"; otherwise "natural strawberry flavored", "with other natural flavor", or "artificially flavored" ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-101/subpart-B/section-101.22), [MSU](https://www.canr.msu.edu/iflr/uploads/Naming%20foods%20with%20added%20flavors%202024-02-23.pdf)). "Real fruit" claims are an active class-action target ([FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator-usa.com/Article/2025/04/15/clean-label-claims-in-the-crosshairs-rising-litigation-trends-zero-in-on-claims-about-real-fruit-natural-flavors-heavy-metals-pfas-and-more/)).
- A front "20g protein" claim requires %DV corrected by PDCAAS; whey's PDCAAS is 1.0 ([Trustwell](https://blog.trustwell.com/using-a-pdcaas-value-to-display-dv-protein-on-your-supplements-facts-label), [Clark Hill on Nacarino v. Kashi](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/ninth-circuit-issues-decision-clarifying-protein-labeling-requirements/)).
- Prop 65 lead safe harbor 0.5 µg/day ([Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2171/2018-05-29-prop-65-get-lead-out)); 47% of 160 protein powders exceeded it in one study ([CLP](https://cleanlabelproject.org/wp-content/uploads/CleanLabelProject_ProteinStudyWhitepaper_010625.pdf)). California SB 1033 would require published heavy-metal results from 2027; final status not found ([Nutraceuticals World](https://www.nutraceuticalsworld.com/breaking-news/california-bill-would-require-protein-powder-brands-to-publicly-disclose-heavy-metal-testing-results/)).

## Business setup costs

- USPTO: $350 per class since 2025-01-18 ([Fish & Richardson](https://www.fr.com/insights/thought-leadership/blogs/new-uspto-trademark-fees-go-into-effect-january-18-2025/)); first action about 4.4 months ([USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/subscription-center/2026/reducing-trademark-pendency-improving-processing)).
- GS1 US company prefix, 10 items: $250 plus $50 a year ([GS1 US](https://store.gs1us.org/gs1-company-prefix/p)).
- EIN free ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)); LLC $35–500 by state ([Bizee](https://bizee.com/llc/state-filing-fees)).
- Product liability: retailers usually want $1M/$2M with them as additional insured ([Insurance Canopy](https://www.insurancecanopy.com/blog/vendor-insurance-requirements)); KeHE $2M/$2M.
- Non-GMO Project: $115 per product plus about $2,000 a year ([SCS](https://www.nongmoproject.org/wp-content/uploads/scs-non-gmo-project-pricing-082025.pdf)). NSF Certified for Sport and Informed Sport: quoted individually; estimates in the low thousands per product ([V5](https://v5ultimate.com/guides/supplement-third-party-testing-compared)).
