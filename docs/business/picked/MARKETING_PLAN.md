# Picked marketing plan: the first 90 days

October 5, 2026 to January 3, 2027, ending the week pre-orders open. The
goal is one number: **enough people on the waitlist to sell out the first
run on pre-order.**

This page is the plan. The material it uses already exists: video scripts in
`marketing/TIKTOK.md`, the Instagram grid in `marketing/INSTAGRAM.md`, emails
in `marketing/EMAIL.md`, the lead magnet in `marketing/REAL_FRUIT_TEST.md`,
product copy in `marketing/PRODUCT_COPY.md`, account setup in
`SOCIAL_SETUP.md`, and what to learn from Bloom in `BLOOM_LESSONS.md`.

## The math that sets the goal

There's no reliable public benchmark for how many waitlist signups buy on
pre-order, and it varies widely. Plan on 10%, check it against 5%, and
replace both with your own number the day pre-orders open.

| First run | Waitlist needed at 10% | Waitlist needed at 5% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 144 pouches (lean path) | about 1,450 | about 2,900 |
| 1,000 pouches (standard path) | about 10,000 | about 20,000 |

**Target: 2,000 on the waitlist by January 3.** That covers the lean run
even at 7% and gives a real signal for the second run. These are targets,
not forecasts. Track the real number weekly and adjust the run size to the
list you actually have, not the one you hoped for.

## The engine

What Bloom proved, done the Picked way (`BLOOM_LESSONS.md`):

1. **You, on camera, most days.** Build-in-public diary, label reads, fruit.
   Nobody else can be the founder.
2. **One series people come back for:** The Real Fruit Test. Read a label,
   find the fruit, score it. Fair, factual, never mocking.
3. **Proof as content:** the grams, the lab, the Lot Book, the blind test.
4. **A say, not just a sale:** First Picks vote on the next flavor.
5. **Many small creators, later:** build the list now; gift once the product
   is real.

## Creators, the Bloom way, with Picked's rules

- **Now to January:** build the list (100), follow, and comment genuinely.
  No asks before a product exists.
- **When pouches arrive:** gift 50 micro-creators in the first two weeks,
  50 more in the next month. Free product, no obligation, "gifted" in the
  caption if they post.
- **Paid:** only after you see who posts on their own. Pay the ones whose
  videos drove waitlist or sales, using `legal/creator-agreement.md`.
- **Budget:** product and postage, about $15 to $20 per creator. 100
  creators is about $1,500 to $2,000 in month one after launch.
- **Brief:** loose on style, tight on claims. They get the approved claims
  list from `CLAUDE.md` in `ai-team/repo-template/`.

## Paid ads: not yet

No ads before launch. After launch, boost only organic videos that already
work (TikTok Spark Ads, Instagram partnership ads with a creator's
permission), starting at $10 to $20 a day. Stop any ad whose cost to win a
first order is above about $22 (`STRATEGY.md`).

## PR: one story, told plainly

**The angle:** the protein brand that prints its fruit grams and posts every
lot's lab results. A local founder, a real number, a public page.

- Local news and city magazines first: business and food desks.
- Food and fitness newsletters second.
- Trade press (Nutritional Outlook, BevNET, NOSH) at launch, with real
  numbers only.
- Never claim press coverage you haven't had. Never pay for "as seen in".

## Never

- Health, weight-loss, muscle, or GLP-1 claims, in posts, replies, or creator
  content.
- Invented numbers: no waitlist counts, reviews, or results that aren't real.
- AI or stock images of a pouch that doesn't exist yet. Concept art is
  labeled as concept art.
- Marketing to under-18s, or school and teen angles.
- A deadline that isn't real.
