**How it closes.** The box is sealed with one fruit sticker across the front
edge of the lid: the same 3 × 2 in oval as on the pouch, printed as a roll
label. Opening the box means breaking the sticker.

**Before you order boxes:**

1. Get the supplier's dieline (Packlane, Arka, and noissue all supply one) for
   a mailer that fits one pouch with a little room. The 10 × 7 × 3 in size
   here is a starting point, not a measurement.
2. Place these faces on the dieline with 0.125 in bleed. The QR code must stay
   at least 0.8 in square and keep its white border.
3. Write the founder note on the card yourself, and sign it. Leave the bracket
   until you do: it is the only line on the box that should be in your words.
4. "[x] oz of cold water" on the card back waits for the final mixing
   instructions from the formula.

Rebuild with `python3 brand/src/unboxing.py` (see `brand/src/README.md`).
