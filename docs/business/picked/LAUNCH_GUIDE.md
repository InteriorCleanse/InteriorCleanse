# Picked launch guide

**The plan in one line:** make one great flavour, pre-sell it to a waitlist,
sell it on your own site, sample it in person, then climb the store ladder one
rung at a time as the numbers allow.

Dates assume you start the week of 2026-10-05. Estimates are marked; sources
are in `RESEARCH.md`.

## Week 1: set up and start the clock

The manufacturer is the long pole, so the request for quotes goes out first.

- [ ] Email the request for quotes (`outreach/01-manufacturer-rfq.md` plus `FORMULA_BRIEF.md`) to five manufacturers: NutraSeller, Makers Nutrition, Nutrablend Foods, Nutracap, Cpack.
- [ ] Search "PICKED" at tmsearch.uspto.gov, then book a trademark attorney for a clearance opinion.
- [ ] Register pickedprotein.com and drinkpicked.com. Claim @pickedprotein on Instagram and TikTok.
- [ ] Form an LLC, get an EIN (free, online, same day), and open a business bank account.
- [ ] Put the waitlist site live (`site/`, see `site/README.md`) with a Klaviyo signup form.
- [ ] Post the first "building a protein that tastes like fruit" video. Film everything from here on.

## Weeks 2 to 8: make the product

- [ ] Compare quotes. Choose on four things: fruit-forward flavour skill, NSF/ANSI 455-2 or equivalent GMP certification, a lot-level heavy-metal certificate of analysis (COA), and MOQ. Price comes fifth.
- [ ] Order bench samples, usually 2 to 4 rounds over 4 to 6 weeks. Start with Strawberry.
- [ ] Run a blind taste panel: 30 people from the waitlist score Picked against Ghost Clear and Myprotein Clear. Ship only if Picked wins on "tastes like real fruit".
- [ ] Lock the formula. Ask for grams of fruit per serving in writing, then print that number on the pack.
- [ ] Hire a label consultant or food lawyer to review the Supplement Facts panel, the "made with real strawberries" wording under 21 CFR 101.22, the protein %DV, and any Prop 65 warning.
- [ ] File the trademark in classes 5 and 32 ($350 per class). Buy a GS1 US company prefix ($250 plus $50 a year).
- [ ] Get product liability insurance at $1M per claim and $2M total. Stores will ask for it.
- [ ] Send final art to a digital pouch printer for low minimums, using the printer's own dieline.

## Weeks 8 to 14: produce and pre-sell

- [ ] Approve the production sample and pay the deposit.
- [ ] Open pre-orders on Shopify to the waitlist, with founding pricing and an honest ship date.
- [ ] Hand out samples in person every week: run clubs, CrossFit and pilates studios, campus gyms.
- [ ] Set up Shopify. Add a subscription app (Loop has a free tier), Klaviyo flows for welcome, post-purchase, and replenishment at day 25, and a lot COA page.
- [ ] Receive the goods with a COA for every lot before anything ships.

## Launch online

1. **Your own site first.** Ship the pre-orders, then ask every buyer for a photo and an honest review. Never write or buy reviews.
2. **Content engine.** Three short videos a week: blind taste tests, "real fruit vs natural flavor" label reads, and the fruit going into the batch. Send product to 20 micro-creators in run, pilates, and nutrition niches. Paid ads only once a first order costs less than about $22 to win.
3. **Amazon (month 3 to 4).** You need the pending trademark serial for Brand Registry, the GS1 barcodes, and a manufacturer that passes Amazon's 2026 third-party cGMP verification. Ask about it in the request for quotes.
4. **TikTok Shop.** Supplements are invite-only and need lab reports less than 180 days old. Build an organic audience first, then apply.

## Get into stores: the ladder

Climb one rung at a time. Each rung has a gate.

| Rung | Who | How | Gate to climb |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Gyms, studios, smoothie and juice bars, independent health stores near you | Walk in with cold samples and the stick pack display. Sell direct at wholesale. Use `outreach/02-local-store-pitch.md`. | 10 accounts reordering |
| 2 | Independents nationwide | List on Faire. Send your own retailers your Faire Direct link, which costs you 0% commission. | 25 stores reordering |
| 3 | Natural grocers | Erewhon online submission; Sprouts new-item portal; local co-ops. Watch for Whole Foods LEAP opening in about June 2027. | Velocity data from rungs 1 and 2 |
| 4 | Vitamin specialty | Build a RangeMe profile, which is how GNC and The Vitamin Shoppe take submissions. Apply to Vitamin Shoppe Launchpad. | Facility GMP certificate and lot COAs |
| 5 | Distributors (KeHE, UNFI) and a broker | Only when a chain requires it and the cost of goods is $16 or less | Margin survives about 25% distributor and 30–40% retailer |

**Walk Expo West, March 2–5, 2027 in Anaheim.** Book meetings with buyers and
brokers before you go. Exhibit in a later year.

**What to hand a store buyer:** a one-page sell sheet with price, case
pack, margin, and barcode, plus the COA, the insurance certificate, a
cold sample, and your sell-through plan of demos and local social posts.
Retailers buy what you prove you can move.

**Recommendation:** launch Strawberry only. Fund Mango and Raspberry Lemon
from the first run's sales, and use the waitlist vote to pick which comes next.

## Never do these

- Claim weight loss, disease, GLP-1, or "clinically proven" anything.
- Say "real fruit" on a flavour whose taste mostly comes from added flavours.
- Put "20g protein" on the front without the protein %DV in the facts panel.
- Ship a lot without its heavy-metal COA.
- Use stock or AI images of a pouch that doesn't exist yet as if it were the real product.

## Five numbers to check every Sunday

1. Waitlist signups, and the share who buy.
2. Orders, and the share that are subscriptions.
3. Cost to win a first order.
4. Repeat purchase rate at 45 days.
5. Store accounts that reordered.
