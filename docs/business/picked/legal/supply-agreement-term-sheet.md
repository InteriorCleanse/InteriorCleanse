# Manufacturing and Supply Agreement: Term Sheet (DRAFT)

Use this to negotiate with a contract manufacturer before paying a deposit.
The lawyer turns the agreed points into the contract. The manufacturer's
standard terms usually favor the manufacturer, so read them against this list.

| Topic | What Picked needs |
| --- | --- |
| Products and specs | Each SKU's formula, ingredient specs, fill weight, packaging, and label are attached as specifications. Changes need written approval from Picked. |
| Formula ownership | Picked owns any formula and flavor system developed for it, including those developed by the manufacturer. The manufacturer will not sell it or a close copy to anyone else. A portable formula lets Picked move to another manufacturer. |
| Quality agreement | A separate quality agreement sets out who does what under 21 CFR 111 (supplements) or 21 CFR 117 (food): supplier qualification, identity testing, finished-product testing, release, records, complaints, and deviations. |
| Testing and COAs | Every lot ships with a certificate of analysis from an ISO 17025 lab: identity, protein content, microbiology, and heavy metals (lead, cadmium, arsenic, mercury) with numeric results. Picked may test retained samples independently. |
| Facility | FDA-registered. Certified to NSF/ANSI 455-2 or an equivalent GMP audit. Picked or its auditor may audit with reasonable notice. Prompt notice of any FDA inspection finding or warning letter that touches Picked products. |
| Pricing | Unit price by quantity tier, with protein, fruit, packaging, and tolling shown separately. A rule for passing on ingredient price changes (for example, whey indexed to a published price) with notice. |
| Minimums and lead time | MOQ per SKU, whether flavors can share a run, lead time from purchase order to finished goods, and what happens if the manufacturer misses the date. |
| Payment | Deposit percentage, balance on release of passing COAs (not on shipment). |
| Nonconforming product | Lots that fail spec are replaced or refunded at the manufacturer's cost, including freight and destruction. |
| Recalls | The parties cooperate. The manufacturer pays recall costs caused by its error. Picked controls customer communication. |
| Retained samples and records | The manufacturer keeps retained samples and batch records for at least [1 year past expiry]. Picked can get copies on request. |
| Insurance and indemnity | The manufacturer carries product liability insurance of at least $[2M], names Picked as additional insured, and indemnifies Picked for manufacturing defects. Picked indemnifies for its label claims and marketing. |
| Term and exit | [1 to 2] years. Picked can leave with [90] days' notice and take its formula, specs, printed packaging, and paid-for inventory. |
| Confidentiality | Mutual, surviving the agreement. See `mutual-nda.md`. |
