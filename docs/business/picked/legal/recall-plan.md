# Recall Plan (DRAFT)

A recall is when a lot must come back because it may be unsafe, mislabeled,
or out of spec. Keep this plan, the contact list, and the lot records where
two people can reach them.

## Before the first shipment

- Every unit carries a lot number and best-by date. Shopify, the 3PL, and the
  manufacturer can each report which customers and stores got which lot.
- Contacts on file: manufacturer QA, 3PL, insurance carrier, lawyer, FDA
  district recall coordinator for [region] (find at fda.gov), payment
  processor.
- Product recall insurance considered with the liability policy.

## When a problem is reported

1. **Hour 0 to 4:** log it. Ask the manufacturer for the batch record and
   retained sample. Put the lot on hold in Shopify and at the 3PL so nothing
   else ships.
2. **Day 1:** decide with the manufacturer and the lawyer whether there is a
   health risk, a labeling problem (for example, an allergen not declared), or
   a quality problem only. Notify the insurer.
3. **If it's a health risk or undeclared allergen:** contact the FDA recall
   coordinator. Recalls are usually voluntary but coordinated with FDA.
   Undeclared milk would be serious for allergic customers.
4. **Notify** every customer and store that got the lot: email, plus a notice
   on the site. Say what the problem is, what to do, and how they get a
   refund. Use plain words.
5. **Recover and destroy** returned product with records of quantities.
6. **Close out:** root cause, fix, and a written summary for the file.

## Mock recall

Once a year, pick a lot and time how long it takes to list every customer
and store who received it. The goal is under 4 hours.
