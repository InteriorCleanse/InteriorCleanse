# Adverse Event Procedure (DRAFT)

Dietary supplement brands must report **serious** adverse events to FDA.
Picked's label must carry a US address or phone number for reports.
[Lawyer: confirm the details below against 21 U.S.C. 379aa-1 and current
FDA guidance.]

## Any time a customer reports a reaction

1. Thank them, ask if they need medical help, and tell them to stop using the
   product. Never diagnose or argue.
2. Log it the same day: date, customer contact details, product, lot number,
   what happened, when, outcome, and any medical care.
3. Ask for the lot number and, if they're willing, the remaining product.
4. Tell the manufacturer's QA team and check the lot's records.

## If it may be serious

"Serious" includes death, a life-threatening event, a hospital stay, a
lasting disability, a birth defect, or something that needed medical
intervention to prevent one of those.

- Submit a MedWatch report (Form 3500A) to FDA within **15 business days** of
  receiving the report, with a copy of the label.
- Send any new medical information received within a year as a follow-up.
- Keep all records, serious or not, for **6 years**.

## Each month

Review the log for patterns by lot or symptom, and share it with the
manufacturer.
