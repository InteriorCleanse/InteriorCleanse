# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (DRAFT, lawyer review required)

Between [Picked LLC legal name], [address] ("Picked"), and [Company], [address]
("Company"). Effective [date].

1. **Purpose.** The parties will share information to evaluate the
   development and manufacture of Picked products (the "Purpose").
2. **Confidential Information** means non-public information shared by one
   party with the other, in any form, including formulas, ingredient
   specifications, supplier names, costs, pricing, customer data, and
   business plans, whether or not marked confidential.
3. **Exclusions.** Information that is or becomes public through no fault of
   the receiver, was known to the receiver without restriction before
   disclosure, is independently developed without use of the other party's
   information, or is lawfully received from a third party without
   restriction.
4. **Obligations.** The receiver will use Confidential Information only for
   the Purpose, protect it with at least reasonable care, and share it only
   with employees and advisers who need it and are bound by similar duties.
5. **Formulas and development work.** Any formula, flavor system,
   specification, or packaging developed for Picked, and any modification of
   one, is Picked's Confidential Information and property, whether developed
   by Picked or by Company at Picked's request. Company will not sell or
   offer a substantially similar formula or flavor system to any other party.
   [Lawyer: align with the supply agreement and any development fee.]
6. **Legally required disclosure.** Allowed with prompt notice, where lawful,
   and only to the extent required.
7. **Return or destruction** of Confidential Information on request.
8. **Term.** Two years from the effective date. Duties for formulas and trade
   secrets last as long as they remain trade secrets.
9. **No license or obligation** to do business is created.
10. **Remedies.** Breach may cause irreparable harm, and injunctive relief is
    available in addition to other remedies.
11. **Governing law:** [state].

Signatures, names, titles, and dates for both parties.
