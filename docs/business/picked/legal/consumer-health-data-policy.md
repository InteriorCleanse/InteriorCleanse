# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (DRAFT, lawyer review required)

Last updated: [date]

Washington's My Health My Data Act, and similar laws in other states, treat
some information about health-related purchases as "consumer health data".
Buying a protein or supplement product from Picked could count. This policy
explains what we do with it. It adds to our main Privacy Policy.

## What we collect

- The products you buy or subscribe to, and when.
- Anything you tell us yourself, for example in a support email about a
  reaction to a product.
- [We do not run quizzes about health goals. If we add one, list it here.]

## Why we collect it

Only to sell and deliver what you ordered, manage your subscription, handle
questions and safety reports (we must report serious adverse events to the
FDA), and keep the records the law requires.

## Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the store for us (store platform,
payments, email, fulfillment, carriers), under contracts that limit their
use to serving you. We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it
with advertising platforms. [Lawyer: confirm against our actual pixels and
tags before any ad pixel is added.]

## Your rights

You can ask what consumer health data we hold, ask us to delete it, and
withdraw consent. Email privacy@pickedprotein.com. We answer within 45 days.
You can appeal a decision by replying to our answer, and you may contact the
Washington Attorney General if you disagree with the outcome.
