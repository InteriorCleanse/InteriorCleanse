# App Name Search — Get-it and Hit-it

Findings from a web search on 10 September 2026. This is a preliminary
clearance check, not legal advice. Before spending on branding, run a
formal search on the USPTO Trademark Search tool and have a trademark
attorney review it. A US filing in Class 9 (software) and Class 41
(fitness services) or Class 45 (dating and social networking) is typically
where these names would be examined.

## Hit-it (fitness dating app)

**Risk: high.** Do not build the brand on this name without legal review.

- **"HIT IT!" is a registered US trademark**, serial 87671455, registration
  5486497, registered 5 June 2018, status live. It covers downloadable
  audio and video featuring group fitness classes and fitness instruction,
  and physical fitness assessment, conditioning classes, consultation,
  instruction, and training services.
- A dating app is a different category, but yours is explicitly a
  *fitness* dating app that runs sessions, classes, and training events.
  That overlap is exactly what an examiner or the mark owner would point
  to. Expect a likelihood-of-confusion refusal or an opposition.
- App stores already carry "Hit it Hard Gym", "Golf Fitness by HIT IT
  GREAT" (also a registered mark), and several "HIT" training apps, which
  further crowds the space.

## Get-it (fitness app)

**Risk: medium.** Probably usable, but hard to protect.

- No registered fitness trademark for "GET IT" surfaced in the search.
- The App Store and Google Play already have several "GetIt" and "Get It"
  apps: an AI list assistant by LogicMind Design, a "Getit" shopping and
  delivery platform, a "Get_IT" app, and a grocery list app. None are
  fitness apps, so store-listing conflicts are unlikely, but discoverability
  suffers: searching "Get it" will not surface yours first.
- "Get it" is a common phrase, so a trademark would be weak and the USPTO
  may treat it as descriptive for a goal-tracking app. You can likely use
  it; you may struggle to stop others from using it.

## Recommended next steps

1. Search both names on the USPTO Trademark Search tool at
   tmsearch.uspto.gov with "live" status, in Classes 9, 41, 42, and 45.
2. Check domain and social handle availability for each candidate.
3. Pick a more distinctive name for the dating app now, before any more
   assets are made. Candidate names below.

## Candidate names

For the **fitness dating app** (replacing Hit-it). Chosen to be
distinctive, short, and to keep the "active, social" energy:

| Name | Why it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Spotter** | The person who has your back in the gym. Warm, immediately understood, ownable as a dating brand. Check for existing social apps. |
| **Rep** | Short for repetition and reputation. One syllable, strong app icon. Check for conflicts; short words are crowded. |
| **Sweatmate** | Says exactly what it is. Playful, coined, easier to register. |
| **Paceline** | Cycling term for riders sharing the work. Suggests moving together. Distinctive and likely clear. |
| **Tandem** | Two people moving as one. Elegant, dating-appropriate, but a common word to search carefully. |
| **Kindred Fit** | Community first, dating second. Softer positioning. |

For the **fitness app** (if you move off Get-it):

| Name | Why it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Getset** | Keeps the "get" energy, adds "set" from sets and reps and "ready, set, go". Coined, protectable. |
| **Ember** | Matches the Ember-on-Graphite palette. Check conflicts; single words are crowded. |
| **Tempo** | Training tempo, pacing, rhythm. Common word, search carefully. |
| **Loadwell** | Load as in training load. Coined, likely clear. |

A coined name (Sweatmate, Paceline, Getset, Loadwell) is the safest path to
a registrable mark. A common word (Rep, Tandem, Ember, Tempo) reads better
but needs a full clearance search first.
