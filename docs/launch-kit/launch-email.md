# Launch emails (Brevo)

Two sends. The first goes out on launch day. The second goes out three days
later and leads with the free checklist.

Before sending, check:

- Every product named below shows `published` in the admin. The three
  downloads are `needs-assets` today. If one is not live, cut its line.
- The welcome email promises "one email when we open, and after that one a
  month at most." Email 1 tells readers the checklist note is coming. If you
  would rather keep the original promise exactly, send Email 2 only to
  contacts who joined after launch.
