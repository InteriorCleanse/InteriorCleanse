# Seven days of short-form posts

One room film per day, cut to 9:16 from `public/video/`. Each day has a
TikTok/Reels caption (under 150 characters, hashtags included), a Pinterest
pin, and one line of on-screen text.

The films show rooms, not products. Captions describe the product. They
never say the product is in the shot. If you have a real photograph of the
product, use it as the Pinterest image or the last frame.

Before each post, check that its product is `published`. Every URL below is
dead until it is. The downloads (days 3 and 6) are `needs-assets` today, and
the art print (day 5) is waiting on your design approval.
