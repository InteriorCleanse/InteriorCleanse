## The Room Reset Checklist (`room-reset-checklist`, free)

**Title:** The Room Reset Checklist: A Printable, Room-by-Room Reset in Under an Hour

**Summary:** A free, printable checklist with one page per room, ordered so the work compounds, plus a fifteen-minute version for the nights you have nothing left.

**Description:**

This is the checklist we actually use. Each room gets its own page, and the
work runs in an order that compounds: surfaces before floors, baskets before
bins, so nothing you finish is undone by the next step. There are two ways
through it. The full reset takes under an hour. The fifteen-minute version
is for the nights you have nothing left and still want the room back. Print
it and keep it on a clipboard, or keep it on your phone and work from the
screen. It is free, and it is the simplest place to start with
InteriorCleanse. PDF, 9 pages.

**What's inside:**

- A room-by-room order of operations

**Description:**

The Considered Pantry is a system built around how you actually cook, not
how a photograph looks. It starts with a printable label set in the
