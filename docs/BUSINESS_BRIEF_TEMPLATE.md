# Business brief

Fill this in and hand it back. Every line the build needs is here; nothing
else is required to start. Lines marked *optional* can be left blank and the
build will say so rather than guess.

```
Company name:            
Domain (own it? y/n):    
One sentence, what it sells and to whom:

Who pays, and how much (price, subscription, commission, retainer):

First ten customers, where they are today (a platform, a place, a list):

Three competitors or reference sites (URLs):

Three words for the feel (e.g. calm, precise, warm / bold, fast, loud):

Colours or fonts already decided (optional):

Existing assets: logo, photos, copy, social handles (paths or links, optional):

What must NOT appear (claims, brands, words, colours):

Payment: Stripe account exists? (y/n)   Email tool? (Brevo, other, none)

Launch date you want:
```

## What happens once this is filled in

The `/business-build` command reads this file and runs the phases in
`docs/BUSINESS_BUILD_PLAN.md`. Every phase ends with a file in the repository
that you can read, and nothing is published, posted, purchased, or sent
without you doing it.
