# Music tracks not vendored

Upstream `/brag` bundles five "Happy Beats / Business Moves" tracks from
ende.app. Its own README says the exact music licence must be verified before
the skill is redistributed, so the MP3s were left out of this repository. The
skill runs without them (it skips the music bed and beat sync when a track is
missing), and the CC0 sound effects under `../sfx/` are included, as is the
cue metadata in `cues/`.

To use the music, install /brag at user level from its upstream
(github.com/latent-spaces/brag) under the author's terms.
