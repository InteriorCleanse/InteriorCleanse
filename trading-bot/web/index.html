How to read this chart
  • Hover (or touch) for prices and New York time, and for what any box or label means.
  • Session boxes span each session's high and low.
  • A gap turns grey when price closes through it. That's an inversion, and the retest of a grey box is the entry Mr. Cash waits for.
  • Filled triangles mark raids on session levels; hollow ones mark raids on swing points.
  • Dashed boxes are order blocks (the last candle against a forceful move); a purple one has been broken and flipped into a breaker.
  • HH/HL/LH/LL label the swings. BOS is a break that continues the trend, CHoCH the first break against it.
  • Turn on premium/discount to see which half of the dealing range price is in.

Trade planner

Work out your stop, size and targets before you place a trade in TradingView or your broker's app — and compare the option strikes you are looking at. This page only does the maths. It sends nothing and places nothing.

Replays the last 30 days of real candles and takes every setup that passes the full checklist, scoring each one in R. Nothing is tuned, nothing is cherry-picked, and a candle that hits both stop and target counts as a loss.
Every strategy Mr. Cash knows, each judged on its own. Only the ICT session model opens paper trades; the rest are opinions. Turn any on or off, see its vote right now, and test it in full on past prices.
The factory tries many settings for a strategy, backtests each on real past prices, and keeps only the few that hold up out-of-sample — on prices they never fit on. The more settings it tries, the higher it sets the bar (a deflated Sharpe correction), so a lucky winner cannot slip through. It also rejects a setting that only works if its neighbours also work — a plateau, not a spike. Nothing here is ever switched on automatically; a survivor is a candidate for later, not a decision.
The vault is the lifecycle every factory survivor enters before it can trade: a passport records its genome, its out-of-sample evidence, and every result it gathers as it moves candidate → paper → shadow → live. Each passport is watched for decay — its edge fading below the out-of-sample floor — and demoted to watch if it fades. Promotion is champion-challenger: a new instance only takes over when it beats the current champion out-of-sample and on paper. Nothing is ever promoted to live automatically.
The replay player steps through the last stored candles and shows, for each one, exactly what every strategy voted and why — its evidence, tick by tick — and the fused decision at that moment. Press Play to watch the market unfold; drag the slider to jump. Nothing here trades; it is the engine's reasoning, replayed.
Six agents on one surface. BOOK reads the order book and the tape, TAPE organises structure, SIGNAL runs the strategy panel and fuses the votes, RISK holds the veto, REGIME classifies the market and re-weights the panel — and PROOF watches the other five and reports how much they have earned. Every panel carries where its number came from, how old it is, and what it is waiting on when it cannot speak. A blind agent looks blind rather than showing a zero. Read-only: the desk reports state and can never place, size or shape an order.
The intelligence layer shows what the engine sees, automatically marked on the chart: structure, liquidity, imbalances, order blocks, ICT setups, the trade map and market context — each one clickable for its full provenance (what, why, when, which module, data quality, what would invalidate it). It is strictly read-only: it explains the engine's decisions and never makes one.
