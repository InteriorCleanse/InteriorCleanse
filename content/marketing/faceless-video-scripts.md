# InteriorCleanse: 30-day faceless short-form plan

TikTok first (@interiorcleanse), reused as Instagram Reels the same day or the next morning. Every video is 9:16, 30 to 45 seconds, captions burned in, no founder on camera. All footage is the owner's own phone footage of their own home, hands, products, book pages, and the printed checklist. No stock footage, no AI-generated product imagery, no other brand's photography.

## Cadence

- One video per day for 30 days. Post to TikTok first; cross-post the same cut to Reels within 24 hours, re-typing the caption rather than pasting TikTok hashtags into Instagram.
- Each video is one idea, one room, or one rule. Never two.
- Batch-film in one afternoon (see the note at the end), then schedule.

## Posting windows (US home and organizing audience)

These are starting points, not measured results. Hold one window for two weeks, then read your own analytics and adjust.

- Weekday mornings, 7 to 9 am Eastern, for the coffee scroll.
- Weekday lunch, 12 to 1 pm Eastern.
- Weekday evenings, 7 to 9 pm Eastern, for evening-routine and closing-ritual content.
- Saturday mornings, 9 to 11 am Eastern, and Sunday evenings, 5 to 8 pm Eastern, for room resets and weekly-rhythm content.

## Recurring series

1. **Room Reset**: one room, surfaces before floors, fifteen minutes. Ends on the free checklist or on one object that earns its place.
2. **One Rule**: a single household rule told in one breath. This is where the five published products live, as the objects that survived the rule.
3. **From the Page**: a page of one of the founder's five books or one of the three printables, partly read aloud over close-ups of the paper. Every script in this series is HOLD-UNTIL-LIVE until the Amazon or Gumroad link exists.
4. **Heat and Cold**: the at-home sauna and cold routine, plain and disclosed, linking to the partners page. No health claims beyond "many people find."

## Filming kit (owner's own home only)

- Phone, rear camera, vertical, 4K at 30 fps if available, otherwise 1080p. Wipe the lens before every session.
- Window light from the side, curtains open, overhead lights off. Film between mid-morning and mid-afternoon so the light is even. No ring light.
- Tripod with a phone clamp, or the phone leaned against a stack of the paperbacks for low tabletop shots.
- Lock exposure and focus (tap and hold on the subject) before every shot so the image does not pulse.
- Voiceover recorded separately in a closet or a room with soft furnishings, on the phone's voice memo app, phone held a hand's width from the mouth. Or a plain, calm TTS voice. Either way, no music under the voice louder than a murmur.
- Products only where the owner actually owns them: the candle, the mug, the tote, the hoodie, the printed checklist, and the book paperbacks. Where a script calls for the art print or the paid printables and the owner does not have a physical copy, the shot list gives a fallback (a screen recording of the shop page on the owner's own site).

## How to batch this in one afternoon

Film on a bright day between about eleven and three, when the window light is even. Do not tidy the house first; the "before" shots need it as it is. Work room by room in the order the scripts visit them (kitchen, entryway, bedroom, bathroom, living room, closet, porch), and in each room shoot every shot that any of the thirty scripts asks for in that room before moving on, checking them off a printed list of the shot numbers. Keep the tripod at three fixed heights (floor, counter, chest) so cuts between scripts match. Then set the products on the kitchen table and shoot the candle, mug, tote, hoodie, paperbacks, and printed pages in one sitting, each on the same plain surface in the same light. Record all thirty voiceovers in a closet in one pass, reading each script twice and keeping the calmer take. Edit in the phone's native editor or a free vertical editor: drop the voiceover, cut the b-roll to it, add the on-screen text beats in a plain sans-serif, export at 9:16, and schedule. Anything tagged HOLD-UNTIL-LIVE goes into a drafts folder, not the schedule, until its link exists.
