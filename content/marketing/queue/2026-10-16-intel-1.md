# Market intelligence, week of 12 October 2026

Five findings. Most sources are brands, retailers or trade blogs, so treat any
number below as a direction, not a fact. The `last30days` skill needs API keys
this environment doesn't have, so this used web search only.

## 1. Fall scents are going smoky, woody and less sweet

Coverage of fall 2026 home fragrance agrees on richer, layered scents over
sugary ones: smoked vanilla, dark woods, smoky amber, and fig with tonka.
Candle sales overall are flat: Circana puts US candle sales at about
$2.5 billion for the 52 weeks to 14 June 2026, roughly level, so growth comes
from taking share with the right scent, not from a rising market.

- [Scentbird, Fall Fragrance Report 2026](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/?p=36016)
- [Makesy, top candle trends 2026](https://makesy.com/blogs/news/top-candle-making-trends-2026)
- [Happi, citing Circana (52 weeks to 14 June 2026)](https://www.happi.com/?p=271290)

**For us:** the Signature Candle is warm cedar, dried fig and a trace of smoke
(`content/catalog.json`). It is already the scent the season is asking for.

## 2. "Reset" videos are a proven format; the short evening version is open

#SundayReset has passed 620 million views on TikTok (Reviewed). The coverage is
almost all the long weekend version; none of it covered a short daily evening
reset. Most sources date from 2025.

- [Reviewed, what the Sunday reset is](https://reviewed.com/cleaning/features/what-sunday-reset-all-over-tiktok)
- [CNN Underscored, bedroom reset routine](https://amp.cnn.com/cnn/cnn-underscored/home/sunday-reset-routine)

**For us:** the journal already has "The Five-Minute Evening Reset"
(`/journal/five-minute-evening-reset/`), which fits the gap.

## 3. Warm minimalism is the 2026 interiors direction

Off-white, sand and clay instead of stark white; linen, wood, stone and
handmade ceramics; fewer, better objects. For autumn, one trade-fair overview
lists home spa, sensory living and warm minimalism, in brown, cream and
burgundy.

- [Setting for Four, 2026 trends for everyday homes](https://www.settingforfour.com/2026-interior-design-trends-for-everyday-homes/)
- [ArchitectGPT, 2026: from novelty to longevity](https://www.architectgpt.io/blog/2026-interior-design-trends-what-sticks-and-what-fades)
- [Messe Frankfurt, autumn trends](https://conzoom-solutions.messefrankfurt.com/frankfurt/en/topic-overview/trend-products/autumn-trends.html)

**For us:** this is the brand's look already (`docs/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`). The mug
and print photograph exactly this way.

## 4. Faith readers want short daily practice

Reports describe devotionals and short daily-practice books as the strongest
Christian category, with "Bible journal" searches above "Bible study guide".
The figures come from promotional releases and an aggregator; no sales data
for Bible colouring books turned up.

- [ChristShare via WBOC, Christian reading trends 2026 (April 2026)](https://www.wboc.com/online_features/press_releases/christian-reading-trends-2026-devotional-fiction-dominance-revealed/article_ecf6b382-c5b5-589a-a286-f968c35e1f02.html)

**For us:** on the Spirit track, lead with the daily devotional journal, not
the colouring books.

## 5. Cleaning cupboards are being edited, not filled

"One in, one out" for cleaning supplies, and rising minimalism hashtags
(#ProjectPan, #SwedishDeathCleaning) per a TikTok spokesperson quoted by the
WSJ. The evidence is thin and mostly brand-written.

- [Gtech, the 2026 cleaning cupboard revolution](https://www.gtech.co.uk/blog/how-to-declutter-your-home-the-2026-cleaning-cupboard-revolution/)
- [WSJ via To Vima, from hoarders to minimalists](https://www.tovima.com/wsj/these-people-went-from-hoarders-to-extreme-minimalists-heres-how/amp/)

**For us:** the journal's "How to Edit a Cleaning Shelf" is ready-made content.
There is no cleaning product to sell yet, so it is a reach post, not a sales
post.
